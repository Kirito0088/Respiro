# PromptWars Virtual — Challenge 6 Evaluation Blueprint & Strategy

This document establishes the strategic architectural blueprint for our submission to **PromptWars Virtual, Challenge 6: Clean Air & Winter Smog Assistant**.

Our explicit goal is a **100/100 score** across all six evaluation parameters (10/10 on each parameter).

---

## 1. Challenge 6 Problem Statement

> **"Create a GenAI-powered assistant that helps families and communities navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution. The application should suggest low-emission daily routines, indoor air quality hacks, and sustainable alternatives tailored to their location and lifestyle, while clearly explaining the direct impact of their choices."**
